The narrator runs on your own silicon — a local llama.cpp server polishing mission packages and AARs under the voice gates: prose only over a locked spec, machine-diffed, validation-linted, GM-triggered. Nothing leaves the machine; OFF is byte-identical to the templates.

Mode

LOCAL spins the model up on demand; OFF unloads it and frees the GPU. CLOUD arrives with the engine.
